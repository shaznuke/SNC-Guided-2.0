# Bundled dependency

`js/vendor/heic2any.min.js` is the unmodified browser distribution of **heic2any 0.0.4**, downloaded from https://unpkg.com/heic2any@0.0.4/dist/heic2any.min.js. Its MIT license is included as `js/vendor/heic2any-LICENSE.md`.

Author/source: https://github.com/alexcorvi/heic2any
Usage and build documentation: https://github.com/alexcorvi/heic2any/blob/master/docs/getting-started.md
The decoder uses libheif; upstream source and licensing information are available at https://github.com/strukturag/libheif and in the heic2any source/build tree. The bundled file is separate from the application and can be replaced by a compatible build.

The optional local HEIC verification used https://raw.githubusercontent.com/alexcorvi/heic2any/master/demo/1.heic. Test photo fixtures and diagnostic screenshots are excluded from the release archive.
